Privacy Policy — Vera Whoop Integration

What this is: a personal integration that lets James Vitanza's AI assistant (Vera) read his own Whoop fitness data (recovery, strain, sleep, workouts) to help track training and health.

Data collected: Whoop data accessed through Whoop's official API under James's own Whoop account, using OAuth tokens stored privately on his own machine. Read-only scopes only.

Data use: the data is used solely to provide James with fitness and health summaries. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention: tokens and cached readings live on James's own machine and can be deleted at any time by disconnecting the integration.
